Questions before the questions

Things people often ask.

What format is this delivered in?

It's a beautifully designed PDF — fully print-ready. We've formatted it to look gorgeous on both US Letter and A4 paper, so you can print at home, at a copy shop, or take it to a binder. No app, no login, no screen required once it's printed.

Do I have to do exactly one a day?

Absolutely not. Some couples do one a day with morning coffee. Others save them for weekly date nights. Some pull a random page when things feel stuck. The structure is a gift, not a rule.

Is this for new couples or long-term partners?

Both. The book opens with lighter, getting-to-know-you questions and gradually moves into deeper territory. New couples build a foundation; long-term partners rediscover each other. Same book, different journeys.

Can I print it?

Yes — that's the whole point. The PDF is designed specifically for easy home printing. You get two layouts: a one-question-per-page format for binding, and a compact foldable format if you'd like to make a pocket-sized version.

Would this work as a gift?

It's one of the most-given gifts we ship — for anniversaries, engagements, wedding showers, and Valentine's Day. The printed version, bound with a ribbon, looks like a small heirloom.
